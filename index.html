“Text me when you’re home.”

Women already look after each other: a text, a shared location, a friend on the phone until the door. Those habits work, but each one is manual, easy to notice, or left on long after the walk.

SORA keeps the habit and takes the work out of it. She picks a mode and taps once. Her circle comes along, and nobody is tracked once she’s home.

One walk, start to finish

Mei leaves a gig at 72nd Street. Home is 13 minutes away.

  1. 10:29 PM

    Walk me home

  2. 10:34 PM

    Walking together

Four modes

Same friend, different nights.

Every mode keeps the same layout. Only the tint and the tools change.

01

Nightwalk

She picks the route and who walks with her. The busier way is suggested when more places along it are still open, and check-ins live on her wrist.

02

Dating & Nights Out

Lia knows who she’s with. “Call me away” and a ride home Lia can see are one tap away.

03

Commute

Quiet by default. Mom hears only if she leaves her usual way.

04

Traveling

Local emergency numbers, a help phrase in Japanese, and a map that works offline.

Quiet by design

Help that doesn’t draw attention.

In a tense moment, showing distress can make things worse. Every way to get help is quiet.

Hold for help
A long press, so a stray tap never sets it off.
Digital Crown
A double press on Apple Watch, without taking out her phone.
Fake call
A believable incoming call gives her a reason to step away.

If she misses a check-in, help builds in three steps.

  1. 0:00

    Silent alert

    Her circle gets her live location. Nobody near her hears a thing.

  2. 1:00

    Audio deterrent

    Her phone and Watch speak a prompt and vibrate, so anyone nearby knows she isn’t alone.

  3. 2:00

    Emergency services

    If she still hasn’t answered, SORA contacts emergency services.

“I’m OK” stops it at any step.

Shared only while she walks

Shared while she walks. Deleted when she’s home.

Location sharing is useful for a walk and risky as a habit. In SORA it exists for the walk itself, and only for the circle she chose.

  1. She starts a walk

    Sharing turns on with one tap, for her circle only.

  2. While she walks

    Her circle sees her route and her check-ins.

  3. She gets home

    Sharing ends by itself. Nobody has to remember to stop it.

  4. After that

    The route is deleted. Nothing keeps tracking her.

After dark

The same friend, after dark.
